# 👥 Controllers: Members & Profiles (`controllers/members/`)

## 🎯 Kaam aur Zimmedari (Work & Responsibilities)
Matrimonial candidates ki complete profile, 7-step onboarding, biodata generation, aur gallery.

## 📄 Expected Files
- `index.js`: Module barrel exporter.
- `admin.js`: Admin 360-degree member view, manual profile edits, VIP tags, freeze profile.
- `users.js`: 7-step onboarding save, profile photo upload, partner preference update, blind biodata PDF download.

## ⚙️ Key Actions & Flows
1. **7-Step Onboarding**:
   - Step 1: Basic & Contact Info
   - Step 2: Religion, Caste & Gotra (Sindhi Cultural Details)
   - Step 3: Education & Career
   - Step 4: Family Background & Lifestyle
   - Step 5: Astro & Kundli Details
   - Step 6: Partner Preferences
   - Step 7: Photos & Identity Documents
2. **Biodata Generation**:
   - Blind Biodata (contact hidden for privacy) aur Full Biodata (for premium members) generate karna.
